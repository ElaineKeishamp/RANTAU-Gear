# RANTAU Gear

Direction: a practical outdoor rental desk, retaining the existing forest-green identity and navigation. This is the initial design direction for the requested improvement.

- ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.
- Forest green distinguishes navigation and primary actions; warm off-white keeps long tables readable; amber identifies operational attention. Red is reserved for errors and maintenance.
- Segoe UI follows native Windows sizing and keyboard conventions. Large figures belong to operational totals, not decorative claims.
- The repeated motif is an equipment register: unit codes, aligned prices, clear condition labels, and dated rental records.
- The dashboard prioritizes overdue equipment and active rentals. Other screens prioritize a table or a form rather than repeating dashboard cards.
- No new logo, photography, fictional claims, animation, or remote font is needed.
- Fixed light workspace is intentional for front-desk use. Dark sidebar separates navigation from records.
- All seeded records are explicitly demo data. Supabase and distribution packaging are deferred.
